Before You Enquire

Common Questions.

These services are academic, career and admission-guidance offerings. They are not psychological or medical counselling services.

Is Student Mentoring a one-time session?

The current SPRYNX model is structured per student per month and includes monthly review, study planning, test analysis, parent update and habit tracking.

Who is the Career Guidance Seminar aimed at?

The current portfolio positions it around Class 10/12 stream guidance, competitive/career awareness, parent Q&A and a school seminar format.

Does College Counselling include preference support?

Yes. The current service scope includes counselling guidance, preference-list support and an admission-document checklist.

Can a school combine counselling with other SPRYNX solutions?

Yes. Schools can discuss counselling alongside SIP, books, FDP, testing or digital academic support depending on their requirement.
