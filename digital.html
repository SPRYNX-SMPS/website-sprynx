Frequently Asked

Before You
Digitise.

The right implementation depends on academic purpose, users and existing school infrastructure.

Do we need to buy new devices?

Not necessarily. The implementation can first be scoped around the devices and connectivity already available. Hardware requirements should be confirmed only after the intended workflow is defined.

Can a school begin only with online tests?

Yes. CBT or online testing can be treated as a standalone starting point before broader LMS or learning-support features are added.

Can LMS and SPRYNX academic programmes work together?

Yes. The digital layer can support testing, reporting and academic follow-up alongside school integration, JEE/NEET, Foundation or other structured programmes.

Are the prices fixed?

The figures shown are indicative values from the current SPRYNX portfolio. Final commercial terms should be confirmed for the required scope and implementation.
