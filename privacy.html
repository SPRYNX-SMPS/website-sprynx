Website Policy

Privacy Policy

This notice explains how the current SPRYNX public website handles information when you browse the site or contact SPRYNX.

Last updated: 13 August 2026
Current Website V1

The present site is a static informational website. Its enquiry forms prepare an email on the visitor's device rather than storing form submissions in a website database.

Contact SPRYNX →

1. Information you may choose to provide

When you contact SPRYNX by phone, WhatsApp, email or through an enquiry form that opens your email application, you may provide information such as your name, mobile number, email address, city, school or organisation, academic requirement, student/class details or other information included in your message.

2. How the current website handles enquiry forms

The current static version of this website does not operate its own lead-storage database. When you submit a website enquiry form, the site prepares a message for sprynx@springmount.co.in and opens your device's email application. The information is sent only if you choose to send that email.

3. WhatsApp, telephone and email

If you contact SPRYNX through WhatsApp, telephone or email, your information is handled through those communication services and through SPRYNX's normal business communication process. Those third-party services may apply their own privacy terms.

4. Information used to respond to enquiries

Information you send may be used to understand your requirement, respond to your enquiry, prepare an academic or commercial proposal, coordinate a meeting, support an existing engagement, or continue a conversation you initiated.

5. Cookies, accounts and payments

The current Website V1 does not provide user accounts, payment processing or a custom login system, and it does not intentionally set SPRYNX advertising cookies. If analytics, lead-storage forms, payment services, LMS logins or other online systems are added later, this policy should be updated to describe those services and their data handling.

6. External links and documents

The website may link to WhatsApp, telephone, email, downloadable SPRYNX documents or other external services. Once you use an external service, its own terms and privacy practices may apply.

7. Children and student information

SPRYNX services include school and student-facing academic offerings. Parents, guardians, schools and students should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information through general website enquiries. Where a programme requires additional student information, the relevant collection process should be explained separately.

8. Security and data minimisation

Only provide information reasonably necessary for SPRYNX to understand and respond to your requirement. No internet or electronic communication method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be revised when the website, enquiry workflow or digital services change. The date shown at the top of this page identifies the current published version.

10. Contact

Questions about this website privacy notice can be sent to sprynx@springmount.co.in or discussed by calling +91 9750 913 953.

Deployment noteBefore adding Google Forms/Sheets, analytics, online payments, accounts, LMS authentication or remarketing, review and update this policy to match the final data flow.
